# Inspiration (screenshots taken 5 Nov 2026)

- **Brasserie Lolita** (brasserielolita.nl): cream paper, brick red type, hand-drawn ink illustration in the hero, double red rule frames for hours and contact, a photo with a framed menu card on top, the printed two-column menu. Gives us: MenuCard, OrariCard, the sticky Prenota pill.
- **Ilario** (ilario.nl): giant outline name over the hero photo, full-bleed red bands alternating with white, white-bordered tilted photos with a shadow, white line doodles on red, the illustrated "Dove trovarci" map in the red footer. Gives us: Cartolina, the red bands, the condensed label face.
- **Linguini** (linguini.nl): video hero of the kitchen, rounded location cards over photos, a soft blue band with engraving-style icons, italic serif headings, near-black footer. Gives us: Disegni, the `cielo` accent, the italic accent word.
