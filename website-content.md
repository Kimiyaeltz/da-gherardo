## 5. The place

- **Location:** right on Piazza dell'Anfiteatro, the oval square built on the Roman amphitheatre (2nd century AD). The restaurant's whole identity is built on this: the logo is an engraving of the oval square with "Da Gherardo" in script in the middle.
- **Seats:** 100 outside, 80 inside.
- **Outside:** tables on the square under awnings ("all'ombra delle nostre tende"), red tablecloths with mustard-yellow paper placemats printed with a sketch of the piazza.
- **Inside:** not shown in any photo. **[ask for or take photos of the interior]**
- **Pizza oven:** a dome oven with the word "Ceky" in brass letters on its arch (photo 06). **[da confermare: what "Ceky" is, maybe the oven maker or a nickname]**
- **Groups:** large groups, school trips ("gite"), birthdays, communions, retirement parties.
- **Aperitivo:** morning and late afternoon on the square: Spritz, wine, taglieri and crostini.

## 7. Brand clues

- **Logo:** black ink engraving of the oval Piazza Anfiteatro seen from above, houses all around, "Da Gherardo" in an old-fashioned script in the open centre of the oval. Only a screenshot crop exists so far (`logo-reference/logo-anfiteatro-from-old-site.jpg`); the original is at https://dagherardo.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/logo-da-gherardo-lucca.png (109 KB). **[ask for a vector file]**
- **Signature shape:** the ellipse of the piazza. It's in the logo and it's the most famous shape in Lucca.
- **Colours in their world:** mustard yellow and ochre (placemats, the current header), wine red tablecloths, black ink (the engraving), white plates, dark speckled grey-blue stoneware plates, warm Tuscan stone.
- **Their photo style:** professional, bright daylight on the terrace, close-ups of pasta being twirled on a fork, hands in shot, house wine bottle and glasses as props. Branded placemats (Coca-Cola, Sprite, Fanta) appear in many photos; crop them where they distract.
- **Tagline candidate:** "Un'atmosfera intrappolata nel tempo".
